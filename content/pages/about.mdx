I build software for the web, with a focus on creating reliable, scalable applications that solve real-world problems. My experience spans full-stack development, blockchain, and AI engineering—from building responsive interfaces with Next.js and developing APIs and database architectures to writing smart contracts and integrating large language models into practical applications. Regardless of the technology, my goal remains the same: deliver thoughtful, maintainable solutions that perform reliably in production and provide an exceptional user experience.

My recent projects reflect the breadth of that work. **Sol Kat Boutique** is a resale e-commerce platform featuring inventory management, eBay price comparisons, and Stripe checkout integration. **BG Remover** performs image background removal entirely within the browser, ensuring user photos never leave their device. **Solana Mint Forge** simplifies token creation on the Solana blockchain, while **Rip Cord** provides a flexible interface for interacting with any EVM-compatible smart contract, exposing its functions for direct execution. Each project has progressed from concept to production, reinforcing my experience in navigating the technical challenges that arise throughout the development lifecycle.

More recently, I've expanded my focus into **AI engineering**, completing Anthropic's coursework on building applications with Claude and exploring the practical implementation of large language models. As part of that process, I developed an interactive mock examination and study guide for the Claude Certified Architect – Foundations certification. The project reflects an approach I consistently bring to learning: applying new concepts through hands-on development and building tools that serve a practical purpose.

Continuous learning is central to my work, particularly in an industry where technologies, frameworks, and best practices evolve rapidly. I balance that curiosity with a disciplined approach to software engineering, emphasizing clean and maintainable code, security by design, thoughtful architecture, and transparent communication.

Whether you're exploring a new software project, looking for a development partner, or interested in discussing emerging technologies in Web3 and AI, I welcome the opportunity to connect. Feel free to reach out through any of the links on this page.
